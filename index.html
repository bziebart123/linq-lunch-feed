Adding it to Skylight

  1. On a phone or computer (not the frame), sign in at app.ourskylight.com.
  2. Pick your frame, then Calendar → Synced Calendars → Sync new calendar.
  3. Choose Calendar by URL. Do not use the Google, Apple, or Outlook buttons.
  4. Paste the link, give it a name, and save.

Skylight checks for updates on its own schedule. A new menu can take a few hours to show up on the frame.

What you will see each day

The hot lunch, the fruit, the vegetable, and any extra. Each day also lists the alternative your child can choose instead of the hot lunch. It is called Bistro Box at the elementary and intermediate schools, Grab & Go at Templeton, and The Grill or Build Your Own at the high school.

Menus come from the LinqConnect public API and are refreshed weekly. Source and setup instructions.
